# Sight words — lists for the owner to proof

These came from research, not from a native speaker. **Nothing here reaches the
children until the owner has marked it.** For each line: leave it (keep), correct
it, or delete it. Add any words you think are missing.

## Arabic — true sight words (said differently from how they are written)

All five carry a long "aa" that the harakat do not show.

| Word | Said | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| هٰذَا | haadhaa | this (m.) |
| هٰذِهِ | haadhihi | this (f.) |
| ذٰلِكَ | dhaalika | that |
| لٰكِنْ | laakin | but |
| اللّٰه | Allaah | Allah |

## Arabic — very common words (recognise on sight)

## Urdu — words that must be memorised

| Word | Said | Why |
|---|---|---|
| خوش | khush (happy) | the و is not said as "w" |
| خواب | khaab (dream) | the و is silent |
| خود | khud (self) | same pattern — please confirm |
| میں | main (I) / mein (in) | one spelling, two words |
